Before you begin

60 minutes 24 core questions 50 core marks Confidence diagnostic
Purpose: this is not an IB exam and is not intended to predict an IB grade. It is designed to identify prerequisite strengths, gaps, fluency issues and misconceptions before AA SL begins. For a better analysis, discuss your results with a teacher or a tutor.
  • Do not use a calculator!
  • For each question, give a final answer and rate your confidence as low, medium or high .
  • Use the optional working box if you want your teacher or tutor to see your method and if you feel you have enough time.
  • If you are unsure, do not search for the answer. A genuine wrong answer is useful information.
