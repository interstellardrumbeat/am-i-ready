Am I Ready? - IB Mathematics AI HL Readiness Diagnostic

A diagnostic tool created by Dr. Domenico Prudenzano to check the prerequisite mathematics a student should have before starting IB Diploma Mathematics: Applications and Interpretation HL.

Before you begin

60 minutes 24 core questions 50 core marks Confidence diagnostic
Purpose: this is not an IB exam and is not intended to predict an IB grade. The 50-mark core checks prior-learning skills that support a successful start in AI HL, including the additional prior knowledge identified for Higher Level. It deliberately avoids scoring students on topics that are meant to be taught during the course. For a better analysis, discuss your results with a teacher or a tutor.
  • Use the calculator instructions shown on each question. Section A is intentionally no-calculator to check the algebraic and numerical fluency expected before AI HL; Sections B and C allow a calculator so that graphing, geometry and applied questions can be approached in the technology-supported style used in AI HL.
  • For each question, give a final answer and rate your confidence as low, medium or high .
  • Use the optional working box if you want your teacher or tutor to see your method and if you feel you have enough time.
  • If you are unsure, do not search for the answer. A genuine wrong answer is useful information.
