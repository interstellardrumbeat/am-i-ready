Am I Ready? - Grade 8 to Grade 9 Mathematics Readiness Diagnostic

A diagnostic tool created by Dr. Domenico Prudenzano for the transition from Grade 8 / MYP 3 mathematics into Grade 9 / MYP 4 mathematics.

Before you begin

60 minutes 30 core questions 50 core marks Confidence diagnostic
Purpose: this is not a graded school test and is not intended to predict a Grade 9 result. It is designed to identify prerequisite strengths, gaps and misconceptions before Grade 9 begins. For a better analysis, discuss your results with a teacher or a tutor.
  • Do not use a calculator! The arithmetic itself is part of what this diagnostic is checking.
  • For each question, give a final answer and rate your confidence as low, medium or high .
  • Use the optional working box if you want your teacher or tutor to see your method and if you feel you have enough time.
  • If you are unsure, do not search for the answer. A genuine wrong answer is useful information.
Answers remain in this page and disappear after reloading. Nothing is stored online or sent to third parties.
