Am I Ready? - Grade 8 to Grade 9 Mathematics Readiness Diagnostic tool

A diagnostic tool created by Dr. Domenico Prudenzano and focused on arithmetic fluency first, then the algebra and number skills that can make the start of Grade 9 mathematics easier.

Before you begin

60 minutes 30 core questions 50 core marks No calculator Confidence + fluency analysis
This is not a graded test. The purpose is to find which concepts are secure and which ones need practice before Grade 9. A wrong answer is useful since it shows what to work on.
  • Do the core test without a calculator . The arithmetic itself is what we are diagnosing.
  • For each question, enter a final answer and choose Low, Medium or High confidence .
  • You may use the working box if you have extra time, to add your working out. Your teacher or tutor can use it to tell whether the issue was arithmetic, method, notation or focus related.
  • Do not search for an answer. If you genuinely do not know, leave it blank and move on.
  • This tool also records approximate time spent on each question to help distinguish knowledge from fluency .
