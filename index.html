Am I Ready?

Am I Ready? is a collection of browser-based diagnostic tools designed to help students check whether they have the prerequisite needed before starting a new class or course (currently only for maths classes). The aim is not to predict a grade. Instead, each diagnostic is intended to identify strengths, gaps, misconceptions, and areas that may be worth reviewing with a teacher or tutor before the next course begins.
